One conversation. Multiple independent safeguards.

The Banana Navy platform separates telecom security, audio assessment, conversational intelligence and decision authority. That separation creates a resilient path from incoming call to approved action.

Secure by designProtection is built into every stage, not added after deployment.
Human-governedEscalation, approval and safe fallback remain explicit operating choices.
Auditable end to endInputs, decisions, actions and system events can be traced.
Reference architecture

Four coordinated lanes, each with a distinct responsibility

The lanes work concurrently where possible to reduce delay. Clear boundaries make it easier to test, monitor and govern each part independently.

1. Telecom edgeProtect and stabilise intake
Approved channelsOfficial numbers, secure trunks and authorised entry points.
Traffic protectionRate controls, anomaly filtering and overload management.
Session controlSecure establishment, routing and continuity of voice sessions.
AvailabilityRedundancy and controlled degradation during infrastructure failure.
2. Audio assuranceAssess the live signal
Capture & normalisationPrepare incoming audio for reliable downstream processing.
Voice activitySeparate speech, silence and environmental interference.
Signal qualityMonitor intelligibility and apply controlled enhancement.
Anomaly scoringIdentify suspicious or atypical audio patterns for review.
3. ConversationUnderstand within policy
Language & intentInterpret the caller's request and relevant context.
Approved knowledgeRetrieve content from governed organisational sources.
Response policyGenerate answers within defined scope and tone.
Conversation stateMaintain only the context permitted for the interaction.
4. Decision & actionAuthorise the outcome
Risk assessmentCombine conversation, signal and contextual indicators.
Business rulesApply thresholds, priorities and deterministic constraints.
Human escalationTransfer or request approval when conditions require it.
Controlled outputsSend only authorised updates, notifications and actions.

Safe fallback is part of the architecture

When confidence, policy or system health falls outside an approved range, the platform can pause automation, provide a constrained response, transfer to an operator or activate a predefined continuity procedure.

Governance controls

Control remains with the organisation

The platform is configured around the organisation's risk appetite, legal obligations, escalation model and information policy.

Knowledge governance

Define which sources are approved, who may update them and when content expires.

Human oversight

Set review points, transfer destinations and operator authority for sensitive cases.

Traceability

Record the decision path, relevant evidence and technical events according to policy.

Access & retention

Apply role-based access, defined retention periods and controlled audit procedures.

Risk thresholds

Choose when automation may continue, when it must narrow and when it must stop.

Continuity modes

Prepare degraded-service and fallback behaviour before an incident occurs.

Evaluation

Test scenarios, edge cases and policy adherence before and throughout operation.

Security monitoring

Connect technical signals to existing operational monitoring and response processes.

Deployment model

Designed around your infrastructure constraints

Deployment choices are made with the organisation. Hosting, connectivity and integration boundaries are documented before the operational workflow is activated.

Connectivity

Integrate approved telecom routes, identity controls and operational networks without exposing unnecessary interfaces.

Systems integration

Connect CRMs, service platforms, official databases and notification systems through governed APIs or event flows.

European operations

Support EU data-residency, access-control and audit requirements within an architecture tailored to the use case.

Operational readiness before scale

Each deployment begins with a bounded journey, defined escalation rules and measurable acceptance criteria. Capacity and automation expand only after the operating model has been validated.
